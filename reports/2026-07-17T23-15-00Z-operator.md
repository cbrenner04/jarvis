   - **#1730 agent-spawn guard is a NO-OP** — `createPreSpawnGuard()` has zero production callers; no v2 spawn path passes a guard, so the E2BIG guard fires against nothing. **Not merged**; seed `agent-spawn-guard-is-never-wired-into-any-spawn-path`.
