## Cross-cutting observations

**Three separate issues are the same "machine-global only" shape** — #3026 (agents), #3150 (idle timeout), #3039 (readyCommand, now likely fixed). A per-project config-override seam would close two open issues at once and is probably cheaper than either alone.

**`paused` emitting no operator incident appears in three issues** — #3372, #3400, and today's `7475c190`. It is one line of derivation policy and it is why stranded work goes unnoticed.

**Silence is the dominant failure mode.** Of the 22, at least six settle with a *successful-looking* signal (`completed`, `terminal:completed`) while having lost or skipped work: #3433, #3417, #3439, #3400, #3041, #3426. That is the brief's opening class — terminal state written on the happy path only — expressed in the issue tracker.
