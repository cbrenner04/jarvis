# Plan Decisions-Ledger Fragment

repo: cbrenner04/jarvis

Plan mode produces specs and intents that grow to hundreds of lines, burying the
load-bearing decisions in narrative. `global.terse` underperforms because it's a
vibe with no shape — and the real pain isn't volume, it's salience: the calls the
author made get lost in prose. Give plan mode a structural shape instead of a
length plea: record decisions, constraints, and assumptions as a ledger of atomic
one-line entries, rationale as a trailing clause, narrative paragraphs banned —
and the entry *count* explicitly uncapped, so thorough planning stays thorough.
