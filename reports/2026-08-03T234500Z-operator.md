## Friction (not seeded)

- `jarvis run list` gives no hint that a settled row has a live successor step. Diagnosing the false
  completion required reading `run log` timestamps against `telemetry.jsonl` to notice `639c40a6`
  started at the millisecond `eabc39a7` finished.
- Managed worktrees have no `node_modules` of their own for a hand gate; the operator borrows the
  primary checkout's by symlink. Jarvis creates one itself during a run, so this only bites on
  salvage.
- `test:v2` in a managed worktree needs the sandbox off (`mkdtemp` under `~/.jarvis`), consistent
  with the existing note about socket tests.
