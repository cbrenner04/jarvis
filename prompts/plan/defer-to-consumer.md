When drafting behavior ahead of its first consumer, prefer deferral over invented precision.
Do not guess answers that belong to the first caller.
Record each such choice inline as: `Deferred to first consumer: <decision> — pin when a caller needs it`.
Extra precision on behavior no current code exercises is a warning sign, not rigor.
