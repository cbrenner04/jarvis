A reminder that "criteria-complete + green gate" is not proof when the changed path is untested.

4. **One operator regression fix (seed 03).** The reviewed-intent impl made *generic* review steps durable (ungated `store.createRun` / checkpoint lookup), breaking seed 00's non-durable review-dispatch tests — green local gate, red CI. Gated the checkpoint on `deferredIntentOutput` (reviewed-intent only) and extracted a helper to stay under the cognitive-complexity limit; verified full suite + lint:md sandbox-off before admin-merge.
